# Hero image brief — Chapter 14: Case: DesignPilot — Personal Brand and Tool

**Subject.** A still-life-style composition in the warm-paper aesthetic of the brand: a flat-lay or three-quarter view of a designer's working surface — a soft-paper sketchbook open to a wireframe, a single steel-blue ink pen resting alongside, a half-translucent overlay of a Figma-style frame visible above the page as if mid-translation, and a clean printed sheet to the right showing a sprint-ready ticket structure. The composition reads as the Figma-comment-thread becoming sprint-board-language, captured at the exact moment of translation.

**Mood.** Considered, calm, taste-driven. Late-morning natural light rather than late-night neon. The image should feel like a designer thinking out loud on paper rather than a tool icon being marketed at the viewer.

**Negative space for title overlay.** Reserve the upper-left quadrant or full top band for chapter title and case-tagline overlay. The composition should be weighted to the lower right so the title sits in the warm-cream upper expanse without visual competition.

**What the teaching image should make most visually prominent.** The *handoff-as-translation* — the eye should travel from sketchbook (designer's intent) to the floating Figma overlay (intermediate artifact) to the printed sprint ticket (engineer's input). That is the thesis of the case made visible. The JH handwritten monogram is present in one corner as a watermark-strength element, never as a logo lockup.

**Palette.** Warm cream backdrop (`#F4EFEA`), warm taupe surfaces (`#D2BFAB`), muted rose for shadow depth (`#D0C2BB`), the single steel-blue accent (`#4E6B94`) carried only by the pen and the Figma-overlay tint, and near-black (`#1A1A2E`) for any necessary line weight. No cool greys. No electric or neon tones.

**Style references.** Considered editorial photography in the register of *Cereal* magazine or *Kinfolk*; product shots in the manner of MUJI's design language; the warm-paper rendering of Notion's brand illustrations. Avoid the saturated-colour-block aesthetic of generic SaaS marketing and avoid the synthetic 3D-icon aesthetic of AI-tool hero images.

**What to avoid.** No literal robot icons. No "AI" sparkle effects. No Claude or Anthropic branding visible. No human face or hand. No code on screen. No saturated brand-blue or neon green; the palette is warm and reserved by design.
