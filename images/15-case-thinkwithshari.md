# Hero image brief — Chapter 15: Case: thinkwithshari

**Subject.** A clean, high-contrast composition rendering the brand's three-color triangulation in service of the *bridging* claim. Compose three vertical bands that resolve into a single horizon: a navy-ink left third (the data infrastructure), a turquoise middle band rendered as a slim suspension-bridge silhouette spanning the gap, an amber right third (the production AI surface). Below the horizon, a parchment-toned ground. The bridge is not literal architecture — render it as a delicate line drawing or wireframe that reads as schematic rather than illustrative.

**Mood.** Considered, technical, slightly editorial. The image should feel like a chapter heading in a thoughtful design quarterly rather than a SaaS marketing banner. Restraint everywhere; no gradients beyond a single horizon transition, no glow effects, no AI-cliché iconography.

**Negative space for title overlay.** Reserve the upper-left or upper-right band for chapter title and case-tagline overlay. The horizon line should sit at lower-third, leaving the upper two-thirds available for typography. The lowercase wordmark *thinkwithshari* may appear once at very small scale in the lower margin.

**What the teaching image should make most visually prominent.** The *bridge as architectural commitment* — the line drawing should read as a deliberately engineered structure, not a decorative arc. Each section of the bridge has visible support points that read as the four shipped systems (Prompt2Mesh, ORBIT, AURELIA, Prismly) rendered abstractly as labelled support pylons or single-point markers. The composition argues that *bridging* is something built, not something claimed.

**Palette.** Shari Ink navy (`#0A2342`) for the left third and bridge primary. Shari Turquoise (`#00B4D8`) for the bridge accent and any data-flow markers. Shari Amber (`#F4A261`) for the right third and the warm horizon glow. Shari Parchment (`#F5F0E8`) for the ground and as the typography background. White used sparingly for line weight only.

**Style references.** The diagrammatic restraint of *Information Is Beautiful* infographic chapters; the architectural line-drawing register of Foster + Partners' presentation graphics; the warm-paper editorial register of *The Gentlewoman*. Avoid the saturated tech-marketing register of generic AI-product hero images.

**What to avoid.** No literal AI/brain icons. No robot figures. No code on screen. No human face or hand. No floating particles or "data flow" sparkles. No competing brand marks (no GPT-4o badge, no n8n logo, no Streamlit mark). The bridge is the brand argument; everything else is supporting structure or empty space.
