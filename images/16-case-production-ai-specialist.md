# Hero image brief — Chapter 16: Case: Production AI Systems Specialist

**Subject.** A precise, diagrammatic composition rendering the *150 → 28* latency story as the central visual argument. Render two parallel horizontal timelines stacked vertically. The upper timeline reads as the *before* state — a long uninterrupted bar in slate grey occupying nearly the full width, with congestion markers (small redundant call indicators) clustered along its length. The lower timeline is the *after* state — a much shorter bar in success-green occupying roughly 19% of the width, with the rest of the row left in clean negative space. Two small numerical anchors — *150s* above the upper bar, *28s* above the lower bar — sized as data labels rather than display headlines.

**Mood.** Documentary-precise, calm, considered. The image should feel like a slide from a senior engineer's post-mortem rather than a marketing hero. The composition is the argument; no decorative flourish.

**Negative space for title overlay.** Reserve the upper third of the canvas, above both timelines, for chapter title and case-tagline overlay. The two timelines should sit in the lower two-thirds, the lower one weighted to the left edge to leave the right side of the canvas open for breathing room.

**What the teaching image should make most visually prominent.** The *length differential between the two bars* — the eye should immediately register the 81% compression as visual fact before reading either number. The negative space to the right of the *after* bar is doing as much argumentative work as the bar itself; that space is what was returned to the user.

**Palette.** Slate dark `#0f172a` for the upper timeline and any heavy line weight. Primary blue `#3b82f6` only as a single accent on the timeline's start markers. Success green `#10b981` exclusively on the lower (after) timeline. Neutral grey `#64748b` for axis labels and secondary annotations. White or very light grey backdrop. JetBrains Mono used for the *150s* / *28s* labels at small scale.

**Style references.** The diagrammatic clarity of Stripe's documentation graphics, Linear's product-marketing infographics, Datadog's APM-trace visualizations, Vercel's deployment-timeline diagrams. Avoid the saturated-gradient hero aesthetic of generic AI-tool marketing.

**What to avoid.** No 3D perspective. No glow effects. No literal AI iconography (no brain, no robot, no sparkles). No human figure or hand. No code on screen as a decorative element. No PositionPulse branding visible — this image is the brand's, not the product's.
