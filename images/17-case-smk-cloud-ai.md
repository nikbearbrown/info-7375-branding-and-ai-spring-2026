# Hero image brief — Chapter 17: Case: SMK — Bridging Cloud Infrastructure and AI Implementation

**Subject.** A clean architectural composition rendering *the bridge as deliberate engineering*. Compose a single horizontal cross-section: on the left, a stylized cloud-infrastructure layer rendered as a precise stack of horizontal slabs (compute, storage, networking, identity) in deep navy, each labelled in small monospaced micro-type. On the right, an AI-implementation layer rendered as a similar stack but composed of agentic patterns (orchestration, reasoning, retrieval, deployment) in the same navy. Spanning the gap between the two stacks: a slim bridge-truss in teal, with three labelled pylons reading as *Roadmap, Tool, Educator* — the three operational executions of the Bridge Builder pillar.

**Mood.** Documentary-architectural. The composition should feel like an isometric diagram from a thoughtful systems-engineering textbook rather than a marketing graphic. Lines are precise, not flourished; restraint everywhere.

**Negative space for title overlay.** Reserve the upper third of the canvas for chapter title and case-tagline overlay. The two stacks and the bridge between them should sit at the lower two-thirds, weighted to centre with breathing room on both sides. The SMK monogram appears once at small scale in the lower margin or as a watermark anchor at lower-right.

**What the teaching image should make most visually prominent.** The *three labelled pylons* — Roadmap (CloudPath AI's user-facing surface), Tool (CloudPath AI's underlying architecture), Educator (the AI Integration Bootcamp Instructor role). The viewer should read the bridge as held up by three independent supports rather than asserted as a single line. The negative space between the two infrastructure stacks is the gap the brand argues it fills.

**Palette.** Deep navy `#0A2342`-adjacent for both infrastructure stacks and primary line weight. Teal accent (verify exact hex from brand) for the bridge-truss and pylon labels. Crisp white or very light backdrop. No additional accents.

**Style references.** The architectural-cross-section register of *MIT Press* engineering textbooks; the diagrammatic cleanness of AWS-architecture reference diagrams stripped of vendor logos; the considered restraint of Foster + Partners' presentation graphics. Avoid the saturated gradient aesthetic of generic SaaS hero images.

**What to avoid.** No literal AWS / Azure / GCP service icons. No literal Claude / Groq / Gemini logos. No human figure. No keyboard or laptop. No "AI brain" iconography. No sparkles, no glow, no particle effects. The image is the bridge argument made architectural, nothing more.
