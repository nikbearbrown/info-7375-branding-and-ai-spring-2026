# Hero image brief — Chapter 18: Case: Full-Stack Receipts — Shreya Kini

**Subject.** A close-up still life of a printed thermal-paper receipt unrolling diagonally across the frame, rendered in clean schematic style rather than photographic realism. The "receipts" carry brand-line items: *$150K+ saved · Panasonic SAP BTP automation*, *100:1 ROI · Madison classification pipeline*, *85% reduction · debugging time*, *150+ · issues classified per batch*. Each item is rendered in monospaced micro-type, dollar-sign and percent symbols visible. The receipt continues off the canvas at one edge, suggesting the list keeps going.

**Mood.** Precise, almost forensic. The composition should feel like an audit document treated with quiet care — not a marketing flourish, not a stylized mockup. The brand voice on the deck (*direct, evidence-first, systems-aware, no fluff, warm*) is the mood the image should carry: rigorous with a faint warmth in the lighting.

**Negative space for title overlay.** Reserve the upper-left quadrant for chapter title and case-tagline overlay. The receipt should sit weighted to the lower-right, unrolling diagonally from the upper-right toward the lower-left at roughly a 30-degree angle, leaving the upper-left in clean negative space.

**What the teaching image should make most visually prominent.** The *legibility of the line items* — every metric should be readable on the receipt itself, sized as data rather than as headline. The visual argument is that *the brand is the line items*, and removing any one of them weakens the brand. The single SK monogram appears once at very small scale at the receipt's header line.

**Palette.** Off-white receipt against a deep violet ground (verify exact hex against Shreya's Violet/Sky/Emerald/Off-white palette). A single emerald-green stroke at the receipt's leading edge or as a subtle accent on the rightmost column. Sky-blue used sparingly, only as supporting tone for any depth shadow. No additional colors.

**Style references.** The considered restraint of editorial-stillife photography in *The Gentlewoman* or *Apartamento*; the diagrammatic precision of Stripe's documentation graphics; the typewriter-document aesthetic of considered print design from Pentagram-adjacent studios. Avoid the saturated marketing register of generic personal-brand hero images.

**What to avoid.** No literal cash-register imagery. No photographic realism on the receipt; render schematically. No human hand holding the receipt. No sparkles, no glow, no particle effects. No competing brand marks (no Panasonic logo, no n8n logo, no Groq logo). No "AI" iconography of any kind. The receipt is the brand argument; everything else is restraint.
