# Hero image brief — Chapter 20: Case: KINETIC_ARCHITECT — Intelligence with Intent

**Subject.** A schematic system-architecture composition rendering the BlameBug pipeline as the visual carrier of the brand's *integrator* claim. Compose left-to-right: a stack of fifty thousand log lines on the far left rendered as densely-packed monospace micro-type fading into the background; a webhook ingestion node, then an n8n orchestration symbol, then a GPT-4 reasoning stage, then a severity-coded HTML report frame on the right. The pipeline reads as one continuous flow with each stage labelled at small scale in JetBrains Mono. The wordmark *KINETIC_ARCHITECT* sits as a single line at the lower edge or above the pipeline.

**Mood.** System-architectural, deliberately compressed, professional-engineering. The composition should feel like a documentation page from a production-systems handbook rather than a marketing graphic. Restraint at every line; nothing decorative.

**Negative space for title overlay.** Reserve the upper third of the canvas for chapter title and case-tagline overlay. The pipeline should sit at the lower two-thirds, weighted to centre with the log-line stack on the left fading toward midnight-code dark. Cyan accent appears only on interactive nodes (per the *cyan = action* rule).

**What the teaching image should make most visually prominent.** The *compression from left to right* — fifty thousand log lines on the left, four-section report on the right, with the pipeline stages rendered as the mechanism that does the compression. The viewer should read the image as *the pipeline is the brand* rather than *the pipeline is illustration*. Cyan markers exclusively on the active nodes; everything passive is muted slate or warm grey.

**Palette.** Midnight Code `#0B1326` ground; Stack Layer `#171F33` for card surfaces; Warm Slate `#2D3449` for secondary depth; Electric Pulse `#4CD7F6` exclusively on interactive nodes (no decorative cyan); Clean Output `#DAE2FD` for the report frame's body fill; Ice Byte `#BCC7DE` for connecting lines; Muted Signal `#869397` for tertiary text. Crisp white only at very small scale on report headlines.

**Style references.** The system-diagram register of Stripe's pipeline documentation; the architectural compression of n8n's own product-marketing diagrams stripped of their brand logo; the considered restraint of Vercel's deployment-graph visualizations. Avoid the saturated marketing register of generic AI hero images.

**What to avoid.** No literal n8n logo, no GPT-4 brand mark, no Gradio logo. No human figure. No code on screen as decoration (the log lines on the left are micro-type, intentionally illegible). No "AI brain" iconography. No motion blur or speed lines. No additional cyan accents beyond the *cyan = action* rule. The wordmark is the only typographic flourish; everything else is system specification.
