# Hero image brief — Chapter 21: Case: Systems That Scale — Gunashree Rajakumar

**Subject.** A clean composition rendering three production-scale systems as parallel horizontal traces stacked on a single graph: top trace labelled *Aiera × Anthropic — 10K+ req/min*, middle trace *Airtel — sub-50ms UPI fraud detection*, bottom trace *Flipkart Big Billion Days — 110+ orders/sec at peak*. Each trace is rendered in clean line-graph style with a high-throughput pulse pattern, all at production-stable cadence. To the right of the three traces, a small inset rectangle labelled *Ask Guna AI* showing a stylized chatbot interface — the brand's embedded portfolio feature — rendered as the fourth, brand-level system in the same visual language.

**Mood.** Production-monitoring-dashboard-precise. The composition should feel like a Datadog or Grafana view of three healthy systems running at scale, plus the brand's own continuously-running surface. Calm, observable, no alerts.

**Negative space for title overlay.** Reserve the upper-right quadrant for chapter title and case-tagline overlay. The three traces should sit at lower-left to centre, the *Ask Guna AI* inset at lower-right, leaving the upper portion of the canvas open for typography. Time-axis labels are absent or rendered at micro-scale.

**What the teaching image should make most visually prominent.** The *parallel cadence across all four traces* — three production systems plus the brand's own live surface, all running at the same calibrated, observable rhythm. The image argues that the brand is the fourth system, not a separate marketing layer.

**Palette.** Deep navy `#020B18` ground; Electric Blue `#0066FF` for the primary trace lines; Bright Blue `#2979FF` for secondary annotations and the *Ask Guna AI* inset frame. White only for very small typography on the trace labels. No additional accents.

**Style references.** The dashboard register of Datadog APM views, Grafana production-monitoring screens, and Stripe's metric-graph illustrations. The compressed-elegance of Vercel's analytics overlays. Avoid the saturated marketing register of generic AI-tool hero graphics.

**What to avoid.** No literal Aiera, Anthropic, Airtel, or Flipkart logos. No human face or hand. No "AI brain" iconography. No flashing or glowing elements (the systems are healthy, not alerting). No code on screen as decoration. No additional saturated colours beyond the two-blue palette. The three traces and the *Ask Guna AI* inset are the entire composition.
