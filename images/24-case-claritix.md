# Hero image brief — Chapter 24: Case: Claritix — Context at the Speed of the Alert

**Subject.** A clean schematic composition rendering the four-stage pipeline as the central visual argument: *Detect → Correlate → Explain → Act*, with each stage as a discrete card and labelled connecting arrows in signal-blue. Above the pipeline, a faint trace of a KPI line graph showing a single sharp anomaly spike. Below the pipeline, a small inset showing the explanation output as a one-paragraph narrative with three small *source-cited* link markers — *TechCrunch · VentureBeat · cloud-status* — visible underneath as the architectural commitment made literal.

**Mood.** Production-monitoring-precise, considered, observable. The composition should feel like a runbook diagram from a well-engineered observability platform rather than a marketing graphic. Restrained, deliberate, no decorative flourishes.

**Negative space for title overlay.** Reserve the upper-right quadrant or full top band for chapter title and case-tagline overlay. The pipeline sits weighted to lower-centre, the KPI trace at upper-left fading into the dark navy ground, the explanation inset at lower-right.

**What the teaching image should make most visually prominent.** The *separation between Stages 1–2 (deterministic) and Stage 3 (LLM-bounded)* — render Stages 1, 2, and 4 in cool signal-blue / teal restraint, and render Stage 3 with a single alert-orange highlight on its border. The visual argument is that *one stage is the LLM, three stages are not*, and the architectural decorrelation Chapter 7 names is rendered before any prose is read.

**Palette.** Navy `#0D1B2A` ground; Signal Blue `#0066FF` for primary line weight, Stage 1, Stage 2, Stage 4 borders, and connecting arrows; Teal `#00CED1` for secondary highlights; Alert Orange `#FF9500` exclusively on Stage 3's border (the LLM-bounded stage); Mist / White `#F8FAFF` for typography and small annotation text. No additional saturated colors.

**Style references.** The schematic register of Datadog APM-architecture diagrams, Grafana incident-response runbooks, and Stripe's webhook documentation. The considered restraint of Anthropic's safety-architecture diagrams. Avoid the saturated marketing register of generic SaaS hero graphics.

**What to avoid.** No literal Datadog, Grafana, PagerDuty, Dynatrace, New Relic logos. No GPT-4o-mini or OpenAI brand mark. No Kafka or n8n logo. No human figure or hand. No "AI brain" iconography. No glow or pulse effects. No additional saturated accents beyond the single Stage 3 alert-orange border. The four-stage pipeline plus the KPI trace plus the source-cited explanation inset are the entire composition.
