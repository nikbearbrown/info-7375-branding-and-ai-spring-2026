# Hero image brief — Chapter 25: Case: vigilixx — Market Intelligence, Decoded

**Subject.** A clean schematic composition rendering the radar-arc logomark as the central object — quarter-arc sweep originating from the lower-left and extending across the canvas — with a single neon-cyan dot positioned along the arc as the *live signal* status indicator. Ambient ranked-signal markers along the arc render at decreasing opacity from the cyan dot outward, suggesting the relevance-ranked output the brand produces. Below the arc, a small inset rendering an executive-brief output with three small line items — *signal source · relevance score · recommended action* — at micro-type scale.

**Mood.** Vigilant, observable, quietly authoritative. The composition should feel like the brand argument is rendered in one elegant gesture — radar sweeping, signal detected, the disciplined shape of attention. Restraint everywhere; the cyan dot is the only saturated detail.

**Negative space for title overlay.** Reserve the upper third or upper-left quadrant for chapter title and case-tagline overlay. The radar arc weights the composition toward the lower right. The lowercase wordmark *vigilixx* may appear once at very small scale below the executive-brief inset.

**What the teaching image should make most visually prominent.** The *single cyan signal dot on the arc* — the brand's central commitment that *cyan equals live signal* rendered at the hero level. The ranked-signal markers fading outward from the cyan dot argue the relevance-ranking discipline that distinguishes vigilixx from raw-feed aggregators.

**Palette.** Navy `#080F1C` ground; Indigo `#4F63D2` for the radar-arc primary line weight; Cyan `#00E5CC` exclusively on the single live-signal dot (the semantic-accent rule); Intel Blue `#1B2B45` for secondary depth; Surface `#2C3547` for the executive-brief inset card. Crisp white only at very small scale on typography. No additional saturated colours.

**Style references.** The schematic register of Bloomberg terminal radar overlays; the considered restraint of Stripe's data-illustration graphics; the editorial-document register of *MIT Technology Review* feature openers when they treat technical subjects. Avoid the saturated marketing register of generic AI-tool hero images.

**What to avoid.** No literal RSS, n8n, GPT-4o-mini, or Railway brand marks. No human figure or hand. No "AI brain" iconography. No glow or pulse effects beyond the natural fade of the radar arc. No additional cyan accents beyond the single live-signal dot. No code on screen. The radar arc, the cyan signal dot, and the small executive-brief inset are the entire composition.
