# Hero image brief — Chapter 26: Case: Apexly — The AI Edge F1 Fantasy Platforms Have Been Missing

**Subject.** A schematic composition rendering the dual-AI architecture as the central visual argument. Compose two parallel vertical channels: on the left, a structured-ML channel rendered as a stack of probability-distribution bars in deep navy; on the right, a GPT-4o-mini reasoning channel rendered as a textual-narrative block in lighter weight. Both channels converge at the bottom into a single operator-dashboard output card showing one driver-prediction line with a confidence percentage badge. A single Ferrari-red accent — the confidence-percentage badge — is the only saturated detail in the composition.

**Mood.** Motorsport-engineering-precise. The composition should feel like a telemetry-room readout rather than a marketing graphic — calibrated, observable, and quietly authoritative. Restraint everywhere; the Ferrari-red accent is the only saturated detail.

**Negative space for title overlay.** Reserve the upper third of the canvas for chapter title and case-tagline overlay. The dual channels and the convergence-into-dashboard composition sit at the lower two-thirds, weighted to centre. The Apexly wordmark may appear once at very small scale below the dashboard output card.

**What the teaching image should make most visually prominent.** The *convergence point* — where the structured-ML probability distribution and the GPT-4o-mini natural-language narrative meet at the operator-dashboard output. The Ferrari-red confidence-percentage badge sits exactly at the convergence as the brand's central commitment made literal: *every prediction includes a confidence score*.

**Palette.** Apex Black `#0D0D0D` ground; Midnight Navy `#1A1A2E` for primary card surfaces and the structured-ML probability bars; Crisp White or near-white for the GPT-4o-mini narrative typography; Ferrari Red `#E8000D` exclusively on the single confidence-percentage badge (the semantic-accent rule). Rajdhani Bold for any large display typography on the badge; Orbitron Bold for the percentage numerals; Inter for narrative micro-type. No additional saturated colors.

**Style references.** The telemetry-readout register of Mercedes AMG F1 garage timing displays; the considered restraint of *Race Engine Technology* magazine illustrations; the schematic precision of Stripe's data-flow diagrams. Avoid the saturated marketing register of generic AI-tool hero graphics and avoid the photographic register of stock F1 imagery.

**What to avoid.** No literal F1 cars, helmets, race tracks, or driver portraits. No team logos (Ferrari, Mercedes, McLaren, Red Bull). No GPT-4o-mini, OpenAI, n8n, or HuggingFace brand marks. No human figure or hand. No checkered-flag iconography. No saturated colors beyond the single Ferrari-red badge accent. No motion blur or speed lines. The dual-channel architecture and the operator-dashboard convergence are the entire composition.
