# Hero image brief — Chapter 33: Case: SpendSignal.ai — Decision Layer for Ad Spend

**Subject.** A schematic composition rendering the four-signal decision framework as the central visual argument. Compose four parallel quadrant cards arranged in a 2×2 grid: upper-left labelled **STOP** (with a small *kill it; burning money* sub-label and a single thin red marker), upper-right labelled **FIX** (with *potential exists; broken* and an orange marker), lower-left labelled **INVEST** (with *it's working; scale it up* and a green marker), lower-right labelled **OBSERVE** (with *not enough data; watch* and a neutral grey marker). To the left of the 2×2, a single ad-data CSV input card with three small data-source labels — *Google Ads · Meta Ads · CRM* — flowing into the centre of the grid. Each quadrant card carries a small confidence-score numeric annotation in monospace.

**Mood.** Decision-architecture-precise, calm, considered. The composition should feel like a strategic-marketing decision diagram from a thoughtful CFO-quality reference document rather than a marketing graphic. Restraint everywhere; the four quadrant colors are calibrated and used semantically.

**Negative space for title overlay.** Reserve the upper third of the canvas for chapter title and case-tagline overlay. The 2×2 quadrant grid sits at lower-centre with the input CSV card on the left, leaving the upper portion in negative space for typography.

**What the teaching image should make most visually prominent.** The *2×2 quadrant grid as the brand* — the four labels (STOP / FIX / INVEST / OBSERVE) read as the brand's central differentiator. The single ad-data CSV input on the left and the four-quadrant decision space on the right argue the brand's input-to-decision pipeline at one glance. Each quadrant's confidence-score annotation is the trust-discipline made visible.

**Palette.** Deep navy backdrop ground; near-white card surfaces for the four quadrants; semantic markers — restrained red for STOP, restrained orange for FIX, restrained green for INVEST, neutral grey for OBSERVE. The deck does not commit to specific brand-palette hexes for SpendSignal at the slide level, so render the four semantic markers at calibrated rather than saturated intensity (closer to Linear-Stripe-Vercel restraint than to marketing-gradient saturation). JetBrains Mono for the confidence-score numerals; Inter for any display typography. No additional saturated colors.

**Style references.** The decision-architecture register of strategic-consulting deliverables (BCG matrix layouts stripped of brand mark; McKinsey decision-frame illustrations); the considered restraint of Linear's roadmap-quadrant views; the schematic precision of Stripe's pricing-comparison illustrations. Avoid the saturated marketing register of generic ad-tech hero graphics.

**What to avoid.** No literal Google Ads, Meta, Northbeam, or CRM-vendor brand marks. No GPT, OpenAI, n8n, or Streamlit logos. No human face or hand. No "AI brain" iconography. No dollar-sign or money-pile imagery. No motion blur or speed lines. No additional saturated accents beyond the four calibrated semantic markers. The single CSV input card and the 2×2 STOP / FIX / INVEST / OBSERVE quadrant grid are the entire composition.
