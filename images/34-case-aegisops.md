# Hero image brief — Chapter 34: Case: AegisOps — External Predictive Operational Intelligence

**Subject.** A schematic composition rendering the *Signal Pulse* logomark as the central visual anchor — an ECG-waveform line traced through a stylized network-sphere outline. To the left of the logomark, a small AWS-RSS-feed input card showing three labelled feed sources at micro-type scale. To the right, three small parallel output cards arrayed vertically — *Cloud Engineer · Developer · Engineering Manager* — each carrying a different micro-rendering of the same underlying risk classification (the one-run-three-views architecture made literal). Below the composition, a thin horizontal alert ticker showing P0/P1/P2 priority codes scrolling at small scale.

**Mood.** Production-engineering-precise, calm, observable. The composition should feel like a network-operations-center display at the moment a P0 alert is being analyzed — vigilant but not panicked. Restraint everywhere; the Critical-Red `#A32020` accent is reserved exclusively for the single P0 marker on the alert ticker.

**Negative space for title overlay.** Reserve the upper-left quadrant or full top band for chapter title and case-tagline overlay. The Signal Pulse logomark sits at centre with the input card on the left and the three audience output cards on the right. The alert ticker anchors the lower edge.

**What the teaching image should make most visually prominent.** The *ECG waveform passing through the network sphere* — the Signal Pulse logomark argues continuous-monitoring at the brand-identity level. The three parallel output cards on the right argue the audience-aware-output commitment. The single Critical-Red P0 marker on the alert ticker is the only saturated detail and signals the brand's load-bearing prevention commitment.

**Palette.** Deep Navy `#0C1A2E` ground; Signal Blue `#185FA5` for primary line weight, the network sphere outline, the ECG waveform, and the connecting arrows; Guard Green `#109E75` for the P2-nominal ticker entries and any success states; Critical Red `#A32020` exclusively on the single P0 marker on the alert ticker (the brand's *P0 only — never in marketing* rule); near-white for the audience-card typography. Sora 700–800 for any display typography on the logomark wordmark; JetBrains Mono for the alert codes and audience-card headers; Inter for body micro-type. No additional saturated colors.

**Style references.** The network-operations-center register of well-designed SRE-team displays; the considered restraint of Datadog APM-architecture diagrams; the schematic precision of n8n's own product graphics stripped of brand mark; the editorial register of *ACM Queue* feature illustrations on infrastructure topics. Avoid the saturated marketing register of generic DevOps-tool hero graphics and avoid the alarmist register of incident-response marketing imagery.

**What to avoid.** No literal AWS, PagerDuty, Datadog, or New Relic brand marks. No GPT-4.1, OpenAI, n8n, or Streamlit logos. No human face or hand. No literal *server-on-fire* or *outage* imagery. No saturated red beyond the single P0 marker. No motion blur or speed lines on the alert ticker — the ticker is rendered statically with three or four entries visible. The Signal Pulse logomark, the AWS-RSS input card, the three audience output cards, and the priority-coded alert ticker are the entire composition.
