# Hero image brief — Chapter 35: Case: SignalScout — Your Taste, on Autopilot

**Subject.** A schematic composition rendering the three-evaluator bandit architecture as the central visual argument. Compose three vertical evaluator columns at upper-centre — *Skeptic · Scout · Operator* — each rendered as a narrow card with a small baseline-score numeric label at the bottom (40 / 50 / 45). Below the three columns, a single contextual-bandit symbol (a small ribbon-or-multi-armed-lever motif) connects them and selects one column's verdict for delivery. At the bottom of the canvas, a single ranked-email-brief output card showing one paper title with one verdict score in JetBrains Mono and a small *taste-self-description* line at micro-scale: *"You reward Scout 2.3× more on items tagged 'agents.'"*

**Mood.** Bloomberg-terminal-considered, dark-mode quietly authoritative. The composition should feel like a research-decision dashboard rendered with discipline rather than as marketing graphic. Restraint everywhere; the Aquamarine accent is reserved for the verdict-bearing path through the bandit.

**Negative space for title overlay.** Reserve the upper-left or upper-right quadrant for chapter title and case-tagline overlay. The three-evaluator composition sits at upper-centre with the bandit selection layer below, the email brief at the lower edge. The tagline *Your taste, on autopilot* may appear once at small scale just above the email brief.

**What the teaching image should make most visually prominent.** The *single Aquamarine path* showing which evaluator the bandit selected for this user on this item — one column glows in Aquamarine while the other two read in Azure Blue restraint. The visual argument is that the bandit's choice is the brand's central act, and the JetBrains Mono numerics on the email brief argue the *numbers earn monospace* rule.

**Palette.** Ink Black `#0B0F1A` ground; Azure Blue `#3A86FF` for the two non-selected evaluator columns and primary line weight; Aquamarine `#00F5D4` exclusively on the bandit-selected evaluator path and the verdict score on the email brief (the semantic-accent rule); Alabaster `#EAEAEA` for typography. Space Grotesk for any display labels on the evaluator columns; Inter for body micro-type; JetBrains Mono for all numeric values (baseline scores, verdict score, the 2.3× description). No additional saturated colors.

**Style references.** The terminal-research register of Bloomberg's market-data interfaces; the considered restraint of Anthropic's evaluation-architecture diagrams; the schematic precision of well-designed academic-research dashboards. Avoid the saturated marketing register of generic AI-tool hero images and avoid the friendly-chatbot aesthetic of conversational-AI category default.

**What to avoid.** No literal arXiv, Slack, or VC-firm brand marks. No GPT, Claude, OpenAI, or Anthropic logos. No human face or hand. No "AI brain" iconography. No paper-stack imagery. No motion blur or speed lines. No additional Aquamarine accents beyond the single bandit-selected path and the verdict score. The three evaluator columns, the bandit selection layer, and the single ranked-email-brief output card are the entire composition.
