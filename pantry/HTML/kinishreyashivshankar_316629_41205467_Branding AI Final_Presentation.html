📖 Story 1 of 2  ·  Hero's Journey
The Engineer Who Needed a Better Question

At Capgemini, I was a solid engineer. I shipped Android apps used by 10,000+ people. But something kept bothering me — my team spent six hours debugging a bug that had already been solved three months earlier. Nobody documented it. Nothing prevented it from coming back.

"I didn't just change jobs. I changed what I believed engineering was for."

I came to Northeastern with one question I couldn't shake: what if the system itself remembered? Then came Panasonic — an SAP BTP stack I'd never touched, a real deadline, a workflow that had to talk to S/4HANA. I had to think in systems, not components.

That workflow saved $150,000+. And the question became Madison — 150+ issues per batch, 100:1 ROI. "What would make this problem impossible to repeat?" That question is my brand.

📈 Story 2 of 2  ·  Rags to Riches
From Zero to Production in One Semester

I started my MS at Northeastern knowing Java and ABAP. I did not know React, Node.js, n8n, or how to deploy an AI pipeline. My first semester felt like reading in a second language — technically correct but exhausting.

"I wasn't behind. I was at the start of a different kind of education."

The Panasonic internship changed everything. A production SAP BTP stack, a two-stage invoice approval workflow, OAuth, role-based access, S/4HANA posting. No tutorials for this. Just real deadlines and real consequences. I shipped it. $150K+ saved.

Then I turned that same urgency into Madison — built in weeks, deployed in production, 100:1 ROI. I came in with almost nothing. I'm leaving with two production systems, a live portfolio, published articles, and a brand that proves every claim.
